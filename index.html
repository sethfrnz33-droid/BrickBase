No shared-purpose I/O

Every dedicated output can work at once.

Our I/O is allocated to dedicated functions rather than requiring users to sacrifice one output to activate another. The sole intentional signal sharing is ignition: eight of the twelve 5 V ignition signals also drive optional onboard IGBTs, simultaneously. Installing the igniters does not consume or reassign the twelve smart ignition outputs.
