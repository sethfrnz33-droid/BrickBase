Before you order

FAQ

Do I get the connectors to build my harness?

Yes. Both models include mating ECU connectors and terminal pins in the advertised shipped price. A complete prebuilt engine harness is not included.

Can I tune wirelessly?

Yes. Both models support TunerStudio tuning via integrated Wi-Fi. Big Brick supports an 80 Hz update rate and captive-portal network configuration.

Does either ECU support direct injection?

Mini Brick does not include GDI software support. Big Brick may be integrated with external GDI driver hardware, but this has not been tested or validated; GDI is not an advertised ready-to-use feature.

Is wideband oxygen control included?

Big Brick integrates an LSU 4.9 wideband controller. Mini Brick needs a separate external wideband controller for wideband feedback.

How do I purchase?

Pricing is listed in Canadian dollars and includes shipping and delivery. Checkout links will be added when the official storefront is connected; this site does not currently process orders.
