01 / THE POINT

WIRE IT.
MAKE IT YOURS.

There's no special MAP pin. Injector 1 doesn't have to be on low-side 1. You've got 10 analog-voltage inputs and 16 general-purpose low-sides. Pick suitable free channels and tell rusEFI what's connected.

Some interfaces are dedicated—wideband, knock, CAN and the motor drivers. That's how it should be.

You don't need to build every future idea into the car on day one. Just leave space in the harness.
