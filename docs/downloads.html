Wi-Fi tuningBoth Brick models provide native Wi-Fi TunerStudio tuning. Big Brick's up-to-80 Hz wireless rate is a developer-provided rating, not a guaranteed logging speed.
USB-C tuningBig Brick provides USB-C as a wired connection option; it can be preferable for consistent high-rate laptop logging. Actual sample rates depend on the ECU and configuration.
Onboard loggingBig Brick's integrated non-removable 256 MB SD storage logs independently of TunerStudio laptop recordings.
