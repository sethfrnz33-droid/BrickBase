HOW COMPATIBILITY IS ASSESSEDNO AUTOMATIC ASSUMPTIONS
Candidate does not mean compatible or tested. A matching cylinder count or a trigger decoder in rusEFI is only an initial reason to investigate. Real compatibility also depends on the exact engine variant, sensor voltage requirements, coil hardware, injectors, firmware feature availability and verified wiring.

Future engine pages will include a separate, validated connector-to-component mapping table and source references. Until then, all engine-side and ECU-side terminal assignments remain unpublished.
