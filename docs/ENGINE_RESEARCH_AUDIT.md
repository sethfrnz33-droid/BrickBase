**Meaning of incomplete:** each engine still needs the exact donor engine code/year, factory wiring diagram and connector terminal views, verified sensor voltages and polarity, matching rusEFI crank/cam decoding, waveform/sync validation, coil driver and dwell verification, injector characteristics, output allocation, and a Brick ECU running test. Not every engine will be compatible using original hardware. No original ECU pin numbers should be copied to Brick without individual verification.

**External source caution:** Links are research starting points, not verified individual-year pinout approvals. A link may document an adjacent engine generation, only a decoder name, or an aftermarket ECU rather than OEM wiring. No bulk link-accessibility audit has been completed.
