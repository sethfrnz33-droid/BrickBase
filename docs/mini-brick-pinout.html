48-PIN / MOLEX 5007620481OWNER-SUPPLIED REFERENCE
Pin 12 requires confirmation. The supplied list assigns INJ2 to pin 11 and then repeats pin 11 for INJ1, leaving cavity 12 unlisted. The table does not assert that pin 12 is INJ1; verify before making a harness.
Physical orientation: Header and harness mating-face views can be mirrored. The connector viewing direction and Brick PCB signal continuity have not been independently verified. Always reference the molded cavity numbers on the matching harness housing.
