**Updated November 4, 2026.** This is a sourced desktop/mobile Engine Finder *research* report covering 237 catalog records. It does not require an installed or running ECU. "Research complete" means all seven OEM control/decoder categories have a documented assessment; it does **not** promise native hardware support, wiring accuracy without model-year confirmation, or installation safety.

Family data and speculative geometry are explicitly qualified. External references are not Brick ECU connector pin assignments. ## Evidentiary limits

A specific OEM manual or supplier technical reference may establish wheel geometry and sensor technology without establishing connector assignments, trigger-edge calibration, or rusEFI software compatibility. In that case the wheel or sensor is documented and its *remaining* control interface questions are marked separately. Factory GDI is unsupported on Mini Brick and remains experimental via external hardware on Big Brick. Original high-resolution optical CAS and frequency-excited cam sensors may require non-native decoding or alternative interfaces. A generic named engine family without an engine code/year cannot be assumed to share one OEM trigger.
