BEFORE A PIN MAPREQUIRED SOURCE DATA
  1. Exact engine code, year and sensor/ignition variant.
  2. Factory sensor and actuator connector identification from a trustworthy service reference.
  3. Crank and cam signal type and decoder support for the selected firmware.
  4. Sensor voltage and ground requirements, plus ECU input availability.
  5. Injector, ignition, DBW and auxiliary-output requirements.
  6. Qualified review of power distribution, wiring protection and final validation.
