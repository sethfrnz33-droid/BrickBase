IGNITION & TRIGGER RESEARCHSOURCE-CHECKED / NOT WIRED
Earlier diagrams are withdrawn as wiring instructions. The generated diagrams assumed ignition types and sensor connections before checking engine variants; do not construct a harness from them.
Factory ignition hardwareDumb/passive individual coils. BMW's MS43/MS45 engine-management documents place the coil's primary-current switching transistor in the DME, not inside the coil.
Brick output class to evaluateSix integrated IGBT-capable channels LS11–LS16 are relevant for evaluation of six passive coils; do not substitute 5 V smart-ignition logic outputs.
Not provenCompatibility of the stock coil current, suppression, dwell, exact coil part number and Big Brick driver characteristics has not been validated.
