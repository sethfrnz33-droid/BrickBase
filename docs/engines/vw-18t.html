Earlier diagrams are withdrawn as wiring instructions. The generated diagrams assumed ignition types and sensor connections before checking engine variants; do not construct a harness from them.
Early AEB ignitionAEB has passive coils and a separate ignition power-output stage. The coils must not be treated as smart coils with integrated igniters.
ATW / AUG / AWM ignitionThe cited service-manual procedures identify coils with internal power-output stages. These are a different ignition topology from the AEB setup.
Other differencesEngine code and year determine crank, cam, throttle and VVT details. A 1.8T family-level wiring assignment is not adequate.
