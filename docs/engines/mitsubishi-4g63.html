Earlier diagrams are withdrawn as wiring instructions. The generated diagrams assumed ignition types and sensor connections before checking engine variants; do not construct a harness from them.
Early turbo 4G63Wasted-spark ignition uses a two-channel external igniter / power-transistor unit driving passive dual-post coils.
Later turbo 4G63Haltech identifies later wasted-spark coil-on-plug arrangements with internal igniters; these cannot be generalized to all 4G63 variants.
Trigger family differencesEarly combined CAS arrangements and EVO 4-on crank/cam layouts differ. Do not label all 4G63 sensors as passive VR.

SIGNALS TO DOCUMENTINITIAL INVENTORY

4 injector channels; factory turbo wasted-spark systems typically use two ignition commands, but external-vs-integrated igniter location varies by generation. CAS/crank/cam signal arrangement and sensor power also vary.
