IGNITION & TRIGGER RESEARCHSOURCE-CHECKED / NOT WIRED
Earlier diagrams are withdrawn as wiring instructions. The generated diagrams assumed ignition types and sensor connections before checking engine variants; do not construct a harness from them.
Factory ignition hardwareCommon OEM LS coil packs have internal igniters (smart coils). The Big Brick 5 V smart-ignition outputs are the appropriate class to evaluate.
Crank sensor classificationGM 24X black and 58X grey crank sensors produce digital Hall-style signals despite common 'reluctor' terminology. They are not ordinary passive two-wire VR sensors.
Generation differenceThe referenced 24X crank sensor uses a different supply strategy from a 58X sensor. Identify the actual crank and cam sensors before wiring; do not use an assumed VR input connection.
