Big Brick hardware

Proteus-based ECU with native Wi-Fi, integrated LSU 4.9 wideband, knock sensing and onboard logging. Features and ratings here are supplied by the Brick ECU developer.

OUTPUTSCOUNTS & FUNCTION
16 total low-side outputs. Six of those same 16 also incorporate IGBT drivers for dumb ignition coils. They are not extra channels.
Total low-side outputs16
IGBT-capable channels6 of 16: LS11–LS16
Smart ignition logic12 × 5 V (IGN1–IGN12)
Switched battery-voltage high sides4 × VBAT (HS1–HS4), separate from ignition
H-bridge motor drivers2

The supplied connector drawing lists LS1–LS10 at 2 A, IGBT-capable LS11–LS16 at 5 A, and HS outputs at 2 A per channel. Confirm exact hardware revision and load suitability independently before production use.
