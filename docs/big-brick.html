Network & tuning

Onboard ESP32 communicates with the ECU over CAN and USB for network-based communications. Wi-Fi tuning is supported through Brick ECU's existing software.

No shared-purpose I/O

Every dedicated output can work at once.

Our I/O is allocated to dedicated functions rather than requiring users to sacrifice one output to activate another. The sole intentional signal sharing is ignition: eight of the twelve 5 V ignition signals also drive optional onboard IGBTs, simultaneously. Installing the igniters does not consume or reassign the twelve smart ignition outputs.

The new 121-pin connector cavity assignments are being finalized. Do not use pinouts or diagrams from previous Brick ECU board revisions for this hardware.

Ignition driver allocation

All twelve 5 V smart ignition outputs remain available regardless of the ignition-driver option. With the optional IGBTs installed, eight of those signals additionally operate eight onboard igniters at the same time. These are parallel drive paths, not eight extra independently timed ignition channels.
