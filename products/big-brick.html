Key hardware

More outputs

16 total low-side channels, including six with IGBT coil-driving capability, plus 12 separate 5 V smart-ignition outputs.

Onboard sensing

Dual piezo knock inputs with onboard sensing and an integrated LSU 4.9 wideband oxygen controller.

Wireless + logging

Wi-Fi TunerStudio tuning at 80 Hz and non-removable 256 MB onboard SD logging.

Engine applications
ENGINE CAPABILITIESOVERVIEW

Up to eight cylinders with sequential fuel and spark, or 12 cylinders using batch fuel and sequential spark. Native DBW, flexible VVT and knock control.

Four VBAT high-side outputs are separate from the 12 × 5 V ignition outputs.
