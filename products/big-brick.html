Network & tuning

An onboard ESP32 connects over CAN and USB. Tune from a phone browser over local Wi-Fi, or use experimental Brick Tuner Suite homebrew on a modded Nintendo 3DS or DSi. The 3DS adds stereoscopic 3D map viewing.

No shared-purpose I/O

Every dedicated output can work at once.

Dedicated outputs can run together. Eight of the twelve 5 V ignition signals also feed the included IGBTs in parallel. Those are not eight extra independently timed ignition channels, and the twelve logic outputs remain available.

See the 121-pin pinout and LSU 4.9 wiring guide before planning your harness.

Native handheld tuning

Brick Tuner Suite on Nintendo handhelds.

An ECU tuner and live gauge display on a Nintendo handheld. Yes, the old one in your drawer.

Nintendo 3DS · Tuning in stereoscopic 3D

The 3DS build has live gauges and a stereoscopic 3D map view, with tuning maps and settings in active development. Some editing workflows are still incomplete.

Nintendo DSi · Full handheld tuning interface

Direct ECU Wi-Fi, live gauges and calibration editing in development. The DSi uses a 2D map view, not the 3DS stereoscopic display. Builds are experimental.

Phone · Browser tuning

Connect your phone to the ECU's local Wi-Fi network. Tune and watch live readings in a browser. No phone app required.

Handheld requirement: A homebrew-enabled (modded/jailbroken) Nintendo 3DS or DSi. Stock consoles cannot run Brick Tuner Suite; it is independent homebrew, not official Nintendo software.
