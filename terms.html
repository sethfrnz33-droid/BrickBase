Essential safety notice

An ECU directly controls engine fueling and ignition. Incorrect connections, unsuitable electrical loads, incorrect configuration, or operation with inappropriate safety systems can cause unintended engine behaviour, engine or vehicle damage, fire, property loss, or personal injury. Installation, wiring, configuration, and testing must be performed by someone who understands automotive electrical and engine-management systems. Stop and seek qualified professional assistance if you are unsure.

03 / One-year limited hardware warranty

We provide a one-year limited hardware warranty against failures caused by defects in manufacturing or components during appropriate, intended use. To request an assessment, contact Brick ECU by email, describe the problem, and arrange to send the ECU to us for physical inspection. We determine whether the fault is covered after examination and may repair or replace a covered defective unit as appropriate, subject to applicable law.

Not covered: damage caused by incorrect wiring or polarity, overvoltage, improper power/ground connections, short circuits, incompatible loads, misuse, incorrect installation, unauthorized modification, physical damage, contamination, or other causes outside an inherent hardware defect. For example, damage to CAN circuitry due to incorrect external wiring is not a manufacturing defect.

We do not promise repair services for damage outside the warranty. In such cases, a replacement ECU may require a new purchase. Return-shipping arrangements and mandatory statutory remedies will be handled according to the circumstances and applicable law; please contact us before shipping anything.

06 / Installation & responsibility

Brick ECU supplies an engine-control hardware platform, not a completed, professionally installed, independently certified engine-management system. The buyer and installer are responsible for selecting compatible sensors, injectors, coils and ignition drivers, designing and checking a suitable wiring harness and protective electrical system, and ensuring the complete system is configured and operated safely.

Protective features in the hardware do not make miswiring harmless. Engine control includes timed fuel delivery and ignition events capable of producing combustion. Errors may result in unexpected engine behaviour, loss of engine control, component or engine failure, vehicle or property damage, fire, or injury. It is essential to understand the system, use appropriate protective measures and qualified assistance, and verify the specific setup before operation.
